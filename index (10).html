Brief biography

I am a Bachelor of Secondary Education student majoring in Science at Taguig City University. My interest in science and curiosity about how the world works inspired me to pursue a path in education. I chose this field because I want to share my knowledge, inspire young minds, and help students appreciate science beyond textbooks. Outside academics, I enjoy exploring new ideas, learning different skills, and engaging in activities that challenge my creativity and critical thinking.

Personal educational philosophy

I believe students learn science best when they are actively involved in discovering, questioning, and understanding the world around them. Learning should not be limited to memorizing facts and concepts but should encourage curiosity, critical thinking, and real-life application. As an aspiring educator, I believe every learner has unique abilities, interests, and ways of understanding lessons. My goal is to create a classroom environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, making mistakes, and learning through meaningful experiences.

Career objectives

My short-term goal is to complete my degree in Secondary Education, pass the Licensure Examination for Professional Teachers (LEPT), and begin my teaching career as a science educator. I aim to develop effective teaching strategies, strengthen my classroom management skills, and provide engaging learning experiences for my future students.

In the long term, I hope to become a competent and dedicated professional teacher who continuously improves through experience, professional development, and lifelong learning. Above all, I aspire to make a positive difference in the lives of my students and inspire them to become curious, responsible, and lifelong learners.

♀Learning Goals

The trajectory I plotted for Field Study 1 and 2.

Field Study 1: Observe

Goal 1: To observe and understand different teaching strategies, classroom management techniques, and instructional approaches used by experienced teachers, particularly in making science concepts easier and more engaging for students.

Goal 2: To identify the diverse learning needs, behaviors, and abilities of students and reflect on how the classroom environment, teacher-student interactions, and instructional materials influence their learning experiences.

Field Study 2: Participate

Goal 1: To actively participate in classroom activities by assisting my cooperating teacher in preparing instructional materials, facilitating learning activities, and delivering meaningful science lessons that encourage student engagement.

Goal 2: To develop my confidence, communication skills, classroom management abilities, and teaching strategies through hands-on experiences while applying the educational theories and principles I have learned throughout my studies.

Expectations

Throughout Field Study 1 and Field Study 2, I expect to gain a deeper understanding of the realities and responsibilities of the teaching profession. I hope to learn how experienced teachers manage different classroom situations, address students' individual needs, and make science lessons more interactive and meaningful.

I also anticipate challenges in classroom management, lesson delivery, and adapting to different learners. Through these experiences, I aim to improve my confidence, patience, creativity, and ability to communicate effectively as I prepare for my future teaching career.

♁Cooperating School Profile

The observatory where the fieldwork happens.

School profile

Taguig National High School (TNHS) is a public secondary educational institution located along I.R.M. Road, corner A. Reyes Street, Barangay New Lower Bicutan, Taguig City. Established in 1980, the school has served generations of learners by providing accessible and quality secondary education. It operates under the Department of Education, Schools Division of Taguig City and Pateros, and offers educational programs that support students' academic development, skills, and personal growth.

Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

As a learner-centered public institution, the Department of Education continuously improves itself to better serve its stakeholders.

Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where:

  • Students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment.
  • Teachers facilitate learning and constantly nurture every learner.
  • Administrators and staff, as stewards of the institution, ensure an enabling and supportive environment for effective learning to happen.
  • Family, community, and other stakeholders are actively engaged and share responsibility for developing life-long learners.

Core values

  • Maka-Diyos – Demonstrating faith, integrity, and respect for spiritual and moral values.
  • Maka-tao – Showing compassion, respect, fairness, and concern for others.
  • Makakalikasan – Promoting environmental awareness and responsible care for nature.
  • Makabansa – Demonstrating patriotism, responsible citizenship, and love for the country.

Organizational structure

Taguig National High School operates under the supervision of the Department of Education through the Schools Division Office of Taguig City and Pateros. Its organizational structure includes the school principal, assistant principals or designated school leaders, department heads, teaching personnel, and non-teaching staff. Each member contributes to maintaining an organized learning environment and ensuring the effective delivery of educational services.

The complete organizational chart, including the names and positions of school personnel, will be presented based on the school's current official records.

School facilities

School facilities play an important role in supporting the teaching and learning process at Taguig National High School. Classrooms serve as the primary spaces for instruction, discussion, and student activities, while specialized learning areas and other school facilities provide opportunities for practical learning and skill development.

As part of my field study, I aim to observe how the available facilities, instructional resources, and classroom environments contribute to student engagement, safety, and overall learning experiences.
